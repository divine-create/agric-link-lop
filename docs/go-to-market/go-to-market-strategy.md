# Go-to-Market Strategy

## Phased Market Entry and Expansion Plan

---

## GTM Philosophy

LOP's go-to-market approach is built on a single principle: **prove it deeply before scaling widely**.

Rather than attempting a broad market launch, the strategy begins with a single anchor client (AgriLink), a single geography (initial pilot corridor), and a focused provider network — then systematically expands from that proven base.

This mirrors how the most successful infrastructure platforms have grown: earn trust with one client, demonstrate results, then use those results to sign the next ten.

---

## Phase 1: Foundation (Months 1–6)
**Focus:** AgriLink Integration & Pilot Market

### Objectives
- Complete LOP platform MVP and integrate with AgriLink
- Onboard 80–120 vetted logistics providers in the pilot corridor
- Process first 5,000 deliveries through the platform
- Achieve 90%+ on-time delivery rate

### Target Geography
- **Primary:** Calabar – Lagos corridor (anchoring on AgriLink's current base)
- **Secondary:** Calabar urban area for local farm deliveries

### Key Activities
| Activity | Owner | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| LOP–AgriLink API integration | Engineering | Month 1–2 |
| Provider recruitment & vetting | Operations | Month 1–3 |
| Driver app rollout | Product | Month 2–3 |
| First live deliveries | Operations | Month 3 |
| Performance monitoring & iteration | Product + Ops | Month 3–6 |

### Success Metrics
- 5,000+ total deliveries completed
- ≥90% on-time delivery rate
- ≤5% dispute/failed delivery rate
- 80+ active providers on platform

## Phase 2: Expansion (Months 7–18)
**Focus:** Multi-Client Growth & National Footprint

### Objectives
- Onboard 5–10 additional platform clients beyond AgriLink
- Expand provider network to 5 major Nigerian cities
- Launch subscription tier products
- Reach 20,000+ deliveries/month

### Target Clients
- SME e-commerce sellers (via aggregator partnerships)
- Food delivery platforms in Abuja and Lagos
- Healthcare supply chain operators
- FMCG distributors

### Target Cities (in order of priority)
1. Lagos
2. Abuja
3. Port Harcourt
4. Kano
5. Ibadan

### Key Activities
| Activity | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Sales team hire (2 account executives) | Month 7 |
| City expansion playbook deployed | Month 8 |
| Subscription plan launch | Month 9 |
| Enterprise pilot with one large logistics operator | Month 10–12 |
| Cold-chain logistics pilot (for AgriLink perishables) | Month 12–15 |

### Success Metrics
- 8+ active platform clients
- 450+ providers across 5 cities
- ₦20M+ monthly GMV
- First subscription revenue recognized

**Focus:** National Dominance & Premium Services

### Objectives
- Become the default logistics layer for Nigeria's digital economy
- Launch premium services (cold-chain, bulk agri-transport, insured delivery)
- Achieve contribution margin breakeven
- Begin planning for West Africa expansion

### Key Activities
| Activity | Timeline |
|---|---|
| Predictive demand analytics launch | Month 19–21 |
| Dynamic pricing engine deployment | Month 22–24 |
| Route optimization algorithm v2 | Month 24–27 |
| First international corridor (Lagos–Accra) pilot | Month 30–36 |
| White-label licensing product launch | Month 33–36 |

### Success Metrics
- 110+ platform clients
- 1,400+ active providers nationwide
- ₦100M+ monthly GMV
- Contribution margin positive

## Phase 4: Pan-African (Year 3+)
**Focus:** Regional Infrastructure

### Target Markets (in priority order)
1. Ghana
2. Kenya
3. Côte d'Ivoire
4. Senegal
5. Rwanda

### Entry Model
- Partner with existing local logistics providers rather than recruiting from scratch
- White-label the platform to regional logistics companies
- Target pan-African e-commerce platforms (Jumia, etc.) as enterprise anchor clients

## Provider Acquisition Strategy

### Recruitment Channels
- Field agents in target cities (primary for Phase 1)
- WhatsApp and Facebook group outreach to courier communities
- Referral incentives for existing providers
- Partnerships with motorcycle/vehicle associations

### Provider Value Proposition
- Guaranteed delivery pipeline (no need to find jobs independently)
- Faster payment (weekly settlements vs. monthly with traditional clients)
- Performance tracking dashboard
- Access to more clients as volume grows

---

## Client Acquisition Strategy

### Inbound
- Developer documentation and sandbox API environment
- Content marketing (logistics efficiency case studies)
- AgriLink success story as proof of concept

### Outbound
- Direct sales to e-commerce platforms and SME aggregators
- Partnerships with business associations (LCCI, Nigerian Association of Small-Scale Industrialists)
- Integration with popular Nigerian business tools (accounting software, e-commerce platforms)
