## About LOP

LOP is not a logistics company — it is the system that logistics companies plug into.

By connecting delivery demand (businesses needing shipments) with delivery supply (independent logistics providers) through a unified, programmable interface, LOP transforms logistics from a fragmented manual process into scalable infrastructure.

Built first for [AgriLink](https://agrilink.ng), it is designed to grow into a pan-African logistics backbone.
