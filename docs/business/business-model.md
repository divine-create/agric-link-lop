# Business Model

## How LOP Creates and Captures Value

LOP generates value by facilitating transactions between the **demand side** (businesses needing deliveries) and the **supply side** (logistics providers with capacity). The platform earns revenue by enabling these transactions efficiently at scale.

---

## Value Proposition

### For Businesses (Demand Side)
- Instant access to a network of vetted logistics providers
- No need to negotiate individually with providers
- Predictable, transparent pricing
- Real-time tracking and delivery visibility
- Simple API integration — logistics as a feature, not a department

### For Logistics Providers (Supply Side)
- Consistent pipeline of delivery opportunities
- Automated dispatch — no cold outreach needed
- Performance-based reputation building
- Access to a growing network of platform clients

---

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Per-Delivery Commission
The primary revenue model. LOP charges a small percentage fee on each successfully completed delivery facilitated through the platform.

| Volume Tier | Commission Rate |
|---|---|
| 0 – 500 deliveries/month | 8% per delivery |
| 501 – 2,000 deliveries/month | 6% per delivery |
| 2,001 – 10,000 deliveries/month | 4.5% per delivery |
| 10,001+ deliveries/month | Negotiated enterprise rate |

### 2. Subscription Plans
For businesses requiring advanced features or high-volume usage, monthly subscription tiers provide access to enhanced capabilities:

| Plan | Target | Features |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | SMEs & small platforms | Basic API access, standard matching, email support |
| **Growth** | Mid-size businesses | Priority matching, webhook notifications, analytics dashboard |
| **Enterprise** | Large platforms & enterprises | Dedicated account manager, SLA guarantees, custom integrations, white-label options |

### 3. Premium Services
Specialized delivery services command additional fees:

- **Expedited Delivery** — Priority routing for time-sensitive shipments
- **Cold-Chain Logistics** — Temperature-controlled transport for perishables (key for AgriLink)
- **Bulk Agricultural Transport** — Specialized coordination for large farm-to-market volumes
- **Insured Delivery** — Optional coverage for high-value packages

## Unit Economics

| Metric | Estimate |
|---|---|
| Average order value per delivery | ₦2,500 – ₦8,000 |
| LOP take rate (commission) | 5 – 8% |
| Revenue per delivery | ₦125 – ₦640 |
| Provider payout per delivery | 75 – 85% of delivery fee |
| Target contribution margin (mature) | 35 – 45% |

---

## Cost Structure

### Variable Costs
- Provider payouts (primary variable cost)
- Payment processing fees
- Customer support per delivery

### Fixed / Semi-Fixed Costs
- Platform engineering and infrastructure
- Data and mapping services (routing APIs)
- Sales and provider onboarding
- Compliance and legal

---

## Competitive Positioning

LOP is positioned as **infrastructure**, not competition:

- It does not compete with logistics providers — it empowers them
- It does not compete with businesses — it enables them
- Its network effect grows with every provider and client added

This neutrality is a key strategic asset: logistics companies want to join a platform that sends them jobs, not one that competes with them.

---

## Path to Profitability

**Phase 1 (Months 1–6):** Focus on AgriLink integration and onboarding anchor logistics partners in the pilot market. Revenue covers early variable costs.

**Phase 2 (Months 7–18):** Expand to additional platform clients (e-commerce, SMEs). Subscription revenue begins contributing meaningfully.

**Phase 3 (Months 19–36):** Regional expansion across Nigeria. Premium services (cold-chain, bulk agri-transport) drive margin improvement.

**Phase 4 (Year 3+):** Pan-African expansion. Enterprise contracts and white-label licensing become significant revenue contributors.
