# Provider Onboarding Guide

## Joining the LOP Logistics Network

---

## Who Should Join

LOP is open to any legitimate logistics operator in Nigeria with:
- At least one roadworthy vehicle
- A valid means of identification
- A Nigerian bank account
- A smartphone capable of running the LOP Driver App

We welcome individuals, small fleets, and large transport companies.

---

## Onboarding Steps

### Step 1: Submit Application

Visit **https://providers.lop.agrilink.ng** and complete the online application form with:

- Full legal name or business name
- Phone number and email address
- Vehicle type(s) and registration number(s)
- Operating area (cities/states you cover)
- Valid ID (National ID, Driver's License, or Passport)
- Proof of vehicle roadworthiness (FRSC certificate or equivalent)
- Bank account details (for weekly payouts)

For **cold-chain providers**, also submit:
- Refrigeration unit inspection certificate
- Temperature monitoring log sample

---

### Step 2: Document Verification

Our operations team will verify your documents within **2–3 business days**.

You will be notified via SMS and email once:
- ✅ Your documents are approved → proceed to Step 3
- ❌ Documents require revision → resubmit with noted corrections

---

### Step 3: App Download & Setup

Download the **LOP Driver App**:
- [Google Play Store](#) *(link active at launch)*
- [Apple App Store](#) *(link active at launch)*

Log in using your registered phone number. You will receive an OTP to verify your number.

Complete your in-app profile:
- Upload a clear profile photo
- Set your daily availability hours
- Enable GPS location permissions (required for matching)
- Add your vehicle details

---

### Step 4: Onboarding Briefing

Attend a 30-minute virtual or in-person briefing covering:
- How delivery assignments work
- Accepting and rejecting orders
- Using the app's tracking and delivery confirmation features
- Payment and settlement schedule
- Dispute resolution process

Briefings are held **every Tuesday and Friday** at 10:00 AM (virtual, via Google Meet).

---

### Step 5: First Delivery

Once onboarded and approved, you will begin receiving delivery assignments based on your location, availability, and vehicle type.

Your first 5 deliveries are assessed as part of a **probationary period**. Maintain an on-time rate of ≥75% to fully activate your account.

---

## Driver App Features

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| **Delivery notifications** | Push alerts when a new delivery is assigned |
| **Accept/Decline** | 120-second window to accept an assigned delivery |
| **Navigation** | Integrated maps for pickup and dropoff routing |
| **Delivery confirmation** | Photo capture at pickup and dropoff |
| **Earnings dashboard** | View completed deliveries and expected payout |
| **Availability toggle** | Set yourself online/offline at any time |

---

## Payment & Settlements

- Payouts are processed **every Friday** for deliveries completed Monday–Sunday of the prior week
- Payment is made directly to your registered bank account via bank transfer
- Minimum payout threshold: ₦5,000 (balances below this roll to the next week)
- You can view your earnings breakdown in the Driver App at any time

**Deductions that may apply:**
- Dispute resolution deductions (only for confirmed provider-fault incidents)
- Platform fee (where applicable for enterprise fleet accounts)

---

## Performance Expectations

| Metric | Minimum Standard |
|---|---|
| On-time delivery rate | ≥ 80% (rolling 30 days) |
| Acceptance rate | ≥ 70% (when online) |
| Delivery success rate | ≥ 95% |
| App uptime while on duty | ≥ 90% (GPS active) |

Providers falling below these thresholds for two consecutive months will be placed on review and may be suspended.
