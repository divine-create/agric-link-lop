## Organizational Philosophy

LOP is built to operate lean. As an asset-light, API-first platform, the team's primary output is **software and operational excellence** — not physical logistics. This means a small, high-quality team can manage significant delivery volume compared to a traditional logistics company.

The organizational structure reflects three pillars:
1. **Engineering** — Build and maintain the platform
2. **Operations** — Ensure delivery reliability and provider quality
3. **Growth** — Acquire and retain clients and providers

## Role Descriptions

### CTO / Tech Lead
Responsible for all technology decisions, engineering team leadership, and platform architecture. Reports to CEO. Owns technical delivery of MVP and subsequent platform evolution.

**Key skills:** Distributed systems, Node.js, cloud infrastructure (AWS), team leadership

---

### Backend Engineer
Builds and maintains core platform services (Delivery Service, Provider Registry, Decision Engine, Notification Service). Writes reliable, well-tested code and participates in on-call rotation.

**Key skills:** Node.js / TypeScript, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, message queues (Kafka/RabbitMQ)

---

### Mobile Engineer
Builds and maintains the LOP Driver App used by logistics providers. Responsible for GPS tracking, push notifications, delivery confirmation flows, and offline-first capabilities.

**Key skills:** React Native, iOS/Android deployment, GPS/location APIs, offline storage

---

### Operations Manager
Manages the day-to-day reliability of the logistics network. Handles provider onboarding, escalations, dispute resolution, and daily performance monitoring.

**Key skills:** Logistics operations, attention to detail, strong communication, data-driven decision making

---

### Product Manager
Owns the product roadmap and works as the bridge between engineering, operations, and clients. Translates business needs into clear product specifications.

**Key skills:** Product thinking, user research, technical literacy, stakeholder management

## Equity Pool

A **10% equity pool** is reserved for employees, advisors, and key hires. Allocation is at the discretion of the founding team and board, with standard 4-year vesting and 1-year cliff for all employee grants.

---

## Culture & Ways of Working

- **Remote-first** with optional co-working in Lagos and Calabar
- **Async-by-default** communication via Slack and documented decisions
- Weekly all-hands (virtual) and monthly retrospectives
- Engineering deploys frequently — no deploy freezes except major events
- Operations team works in shifts to cover 7:00 AM – 9:00 PM daily (Phase 1)
