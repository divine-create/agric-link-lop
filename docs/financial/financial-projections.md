# Financial Projections

## 5-Year Financial Model & Unit Economics

> **Note:** All figures are projections based on market assumptions and are subject to revision as real operational data becomes available. Currency in Nigerian Naira (₦) unless otherwise stated.

---

## Key Assumptions

| Assumption | Value |
|---|---|
| Average deliveries per client/month (SME) | 150 |
| Average deliveries per client/month (Enterprise) | 3,000+ |
| Average delivery fee (paid by business) | ₦4,500 |
| LOP commission rate (blended) | 6% |
| Provider payout rate | 80% of delivery fee |
| Monthly platform infrastructure cost (Year 1) | ₦4,500,000 |
| Monthly team cost (Year 1) | ₦8,000,000 |
